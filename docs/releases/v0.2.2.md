**Search signs, advanced search, and pausing the reading. For testers.**

- New signs in the search box: `-word` leaves out the files with that word; `a | b` finds either; `"a b"~10` finds the words within 10 words of each other; `( )` groups; `name:` looks in file names only. They join the quotes and the star of earlier versions.
- "Advanced search", under the search box, writes the signs for you as you fill its fields; "How to search" lists them with examples you can click.
- The reading of the books can be paused and resumed, even after closing Mulu.
- The top folder of a library now opens and closes like the others, and the count window has a × to close it.

Windows: `Mulu_0.2.2_x64-setup.exe` (not digitally signed yet). macOS (experimental): `Mulu_0.2.2_universal.dmg`. Test script: [docs/testing/v0.2.2.md](https://github.com/miguelbconde/mulu/blob/main/docs/testing/v0.2.2.md).

**Sinais de busca, busca avançada e pausa da leitura.** Novos sinais: `-palavra` (sem), `a | b` (uma ou outra), `"a b"~10` (perto), `( )` (agrupar), `name:` (só no nome do arquivo). A "Busca avançada" escreve os sinais por você; "Como buscar" os explica com exemplos clicáveis. A leitura pode ser pausada e retomada, mesmo depois de fechar o Mulu.

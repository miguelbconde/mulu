**Fixes for scanned books, and "Read faster". For testers.**

- Scanned books with a text layer were reported as "too large": the PDF engine kept every page in memory. Now it is reopened every 25 pages (1.1 GB → 180 MB in our test).
- While the library is being read, "Read faster" switches to full speed (all processors but one); "Back to background reading" returns to the gentle pace.

Windows: `Mulu_0.1.3_x64-setup.exe` (not digitally signed yet). macOS (experimental): `Mulu_0.1.3_universal.dmg`. Test script: [docs/testing/v0.1.3.md](https://github.com/miguelbconde/mulu/blob/main/docs/testing/v0.1.3.md).

# Test script: version 0.2.2

In the test account. Install over 0.2.1: do not uninstall it.

1. **Update.** Install `Mulu_0.2.2_x64-setup.exe` from https://github.com/miguelbconde/mulu/releases/tag/v0.2.2 and open Mulu. Expected: no reading again; the panel says "No changes since the last check". The library's top folder starts closed and opens with its arrow.
2. **Same search.** Search `homero`: expected 8,268 occurrences in 574 files, as in 0.2.1.
3. **Signs, typed.** Search each of these and note the number of occurrences, of files and the time:
   `homero vergílio` · `homero | vergílio` · `homero -vergílio` · `"homero vergílio"~10` · `"ilíada odisseia"~5` · `(homero | vergílio) épico` · `name:homero` · `homero E vergílio`.
   Expected: `|` finds more files than the two words together; `-` fewer than `homero` alone; in `~10` every passage shown has the other word close by; `name:homero` lists only files with "homero" in the name; `homero E vergílio` searches the word "e" too (capitals are not signs).
4. **Advanced search.** Click "Advanced search" under the search box. Fill "All these words" with `homero`, "None of these words" with `vergílio`, then "These words near each other" with `aquiles heitor` within 25 words. Watch the search box write the signs. Click "Search". Then "Clear".
5. **How to search.** Click "How to search". Click two examples: each one runs.
6. **Only without.** Search `-homero`: expected nothing found, with a line explaining that a word to look for is needed.
7. **Pause.** Add a library with a few hundred books ("+ Add a library", for example a copy of a course folder). While it is being read, click "Pause the reading": the reading stops within a few seconds and the screen says how many were read. Search a word: results come from what was read. Close Mulu, open it again: still paused. Click "Resume the reading": it goes on from where it stopped. Take that library out at the end.
8. **Small fixes.** The "or the whole computer" window has a × at the top that closes it.
9. Note any warning from Windows.

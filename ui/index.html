Welcome to Mulu.

Choose the folder where you keep your books. Mulu reads them and lets you search every word inside, on this computer only.

(recommended)

Your home folder and your other disks. System, program, hidden and temporary folders are left out.

Mulu only reads your files: it never changes, moves or deletes them.

Promises

  • Works entirely offline. No account, no ads, no data collection.
  • Only reads your files. Nothing is changed without your confirmation.
  • Free software, under the GNU GPL, version 3 or later.

Type a word, a name or a concept and press Enter.

Use quotes for an exact phrase ("olhos de ressaca") and a star for the beginning of a word (melancol*).

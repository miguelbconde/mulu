# mulu
Mulu (目录): an open-source digital librarian for Windows. Organize and search your personal library of books and documents with fast full-text search, fully offline, no telemetry.

Mulu (目录, Chinese for "catalog") is an open-source digital librarian for Windows, designed as a lightweight alternative to DocFetcher. It indexes the folders you choose and finds any word or phrase inside your books and documents in a fraction of a second: PDF, EPUB, MOBI, DJVU, DOCX, ODT, RTF, TXT, and more.

Beyond full-text search, Mulu helps you keep your library in order: filters by author, year, and format, tags, duplicate detection, and standardized renaming, always with a preview and an undo option.

The project rests on three commitments: privacy (it works entirely offline, with no account, ads, or data collection), integrity (your files are only read; nothing is changed without explicit confirmation), and openness (free software, with documented code and decisions).
